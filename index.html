Philosophy

True luxury never needs announcing.

We believe the most impressive speed is the kind you cannot hear. AURUM is built on the conviction that silence, proportion and the right materials speak louder than figures on paper.

Each car is hand-assembled in Modena, spending more than 900 hours in the atelier before it leaves. No two AURUMs are ever truly alike.
