Since 1974

Five decades
in pursuit of stillness.

AURUM was born of a simple conviction: that speed and stillness are not opposites. From the beginning, we chose the quieter road.

Origin

From a small workshop in Modena.

In 1974, Aurelio Moretti opened a small workshop on Via dei Motori, Modena. He did not set out to build the fastest car in the world. He set out to build a car that makes people stop talking, simply to listen.

Half a century later, that principle is unchanged. AURUM remains an atelier that refuses mass production, refuses unnecessary noise and refuses to compromise on the details you never see.

Timeline

Our journey.

1974

Fondazione

Aurelio Moretti opens the AURUM workshop in Modena with three mechanics and a single principle: simplicity is the highest form of luxury.

1988

Prima Vittoria

A first race victory at Imola. Not through the greatest power, but through consistency and near-perfect balance.

2001

L'Atelier

The personalisation atelier opens. From then on, every client sits with a designer to choose materials, colours and cabin details.

2014

Carbonio

The first full carbon monocoque generation. Weight falls sharply while structural rigidity rises — the foundation of every track-focused AURUM since.

2021

Elettrico

The electrification division is formed. Not to follow a trend, but to prove that electric power can be delivered with AURUM's characteristic restraint.

2025

Orizzonte

A new generation of the AURUM line-up is introduced: a calmer design language, more honest materials and a more intimate driving experience.

Values

Four principles, unchanged.

These principles have not changed since 1974, and will not change for as long as AURUM is assembled by hand.

01

Misure

Proportion before decoration. Every line must have a reason, and nothing is in excess.

02

Materia

Materials that age beautifully. Leather, wool, carbon and wood chosen to last for decades.

03

Silenzio

Stillness as a form of speed. We remove noise so that only the essence remains.

04

Tempo

Time as an ingredient. More than 900 hours of hand assembly is part of the luxury itself.
